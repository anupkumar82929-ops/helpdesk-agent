# Acme Corp IT Asset Policy

## Laptop refresh
Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Eligibility is based on the assigned-on date recorded in the asset register. Warranty covers hardware faults for 3 years from the assignment date.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen device to it-helpdesk@acme.example within 24 hours. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. For theft, also file a police report and share the report number with IT.

## Repairs and replacements
Raise a repair request with the IT helpdesk. Loaner laptops are available while a device is under repair, subject to stock. Damage caused by negligence may be charged to the employee's cost centre.

## Personal devices
Personal laptops may not be used to access production systems. Personal mobiles may access email only through the managed work profile.

## Software installation
Only software from the approved software catalogue may be installed. Requests for other software need manager and security approval.

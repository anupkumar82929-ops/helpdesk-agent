# Acme Corp Leave Policy

## Leave entitlements
Every full-time employee receives 18 days of annual leave, 8 days of casual leave and 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. Leave accrues from 1 January and is credited upfront.

## Carry forward
Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next calendar year. Casual and sick leave do not carry forward and lapse on 31 December. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March.

## Applying for leave and notice period
Leave must be applied for in the HR portal. Notice required: 1-2 days of leave needs 2 working days notice; 3 or more consecutive days needs 2 weeks notice and manager approval. Sick leave can be applied for on the same day. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave. Paternity leave is 10 working days of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.

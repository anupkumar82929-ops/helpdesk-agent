# Acme Corp Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule
Employees follow a hybrid schedule: at least 3 days per week in the office and up to 2 days working from home. Teams choose their anchor days; managers publish them at the start of each quarter.

## Work from anywhere
Employees may work from a location other than their base city for up to 15 days per calendar year, with prior written manager approval. Work from outside India is not covered by this policy and needs HR and Finance approval for tax and compliance reasons.

## Home office allowance
A one-time home office allowance of INR 15,000 is available after probation for a desk, chair or monitor. Submit invoices through the expense portal within 30 days of purchase.

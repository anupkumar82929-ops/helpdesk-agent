# Acme Corp IT Helpdesk FAQ

## Support hours and SLAs
The IT helpdesk is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST at it-helpdesk@acme.example. Priority 1 issues (no access to systems) are answered within 1 hour. Priority 2 issues are answered within 4 working hours. Priority 3 requests within 2 working days.

## Password reset
Use the self-service portal to reset your password. If you are locked out and cannot use the portal, contact the helpdesk, who will verify your identity before resetting.

## Wi-Fi
Employees connect to the ACME-Staff network with their company credentials. Guests use ACME-Guest, with a daily code from reception.

## New joiner setup
New joiners receive a laptop, email account and access to HR and expense portals on day one. Contact the helpdesk if any access is missing.

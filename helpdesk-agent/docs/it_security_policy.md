# Acme Corp IT Security Policy

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and must not reuse any of your last 10 passwords. Passwords expire every 180 days. Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff; the IT helpdesk will never ask for it.

## Multi-factor authentication
MFA is mandatory for email, VPN and all internal systems. Use the company authenticator app. If you lose your MFA device, contact it-helpdesk@acme.example to have it reset after identity verification.

## Phishing and incidents
Report suspicious emails using the Report Phishing button in your mail client, or forward them to security@acme.example. Report any suspected account compromise to the IT helpdesk immediately.

## VPN
The VPN is required when accessing internal systems from outside the office network. Install the company VPN client from the self-service portal.
